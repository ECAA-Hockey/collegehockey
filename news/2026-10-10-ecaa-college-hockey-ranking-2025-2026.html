ECAA publishes the College Hockey Ranking 2025/2026

ULLH leads Europe, NCAA Division I leads the world.

ECAA · 10 October 2026

The European College Athletics Association (ECAA) today published the College Hockey Ranking 2025/2026, its annual ranking of men's university ice hockey competitions. This first edition compares 16 competitions from 12 countries in two categories: Europe, which includes European competitions only, and World, which includes all of them.

Europe: ULLH clearly ahead

The Czech Univerzitní liga ledního hokeje (ULLH) leads the European ranking with 92.5 points. It tops four of the five criteria in Europe: the longest regular season (26 games per team), live statistics with every game streamed, the highest average attendance (607) and the biggest single crowd of the season, 9,319 at the Battle of Prague.

Finland's FCAA finished second with 79.6 points. It scores highest of all competitions for the number of teams per inhabitant, with 25 teams in a country of 5.6 million, offers live statistics for every game, averaged 577 spectators per game and drew a season-best 2,031 in Lappeenranta. Sweden's SUHL (65.7) edges Slovakia's Univerzitná liga (65.6), which reached fourth place in its very first season.

World: NCAA Division I on top, ULLH second

NCAA Division I leads the World ranking with 93.8 points, driven by a 34-game regular season, the highest average attendance and the season's biggest crowd: 74,575 at Beaver Stadium on 31 January 2026, when Michigan State beat Penn State 5–4 in overtime. ULLH is second in the world with 78.1 points, ahead of ACHA Division 1 (76.7), which drew 6,302 fans to the NC State v North Carolina Governor's Cup in Raleigh, and NCAA Division III (75.8). Canada's U SPORTS is fifth (74.3) and FCAA seventh (69.9).

How the ranking works

Four criteria describe the competition itself and carry 22.5 % each: regular-season games per team, the quality of live statistics, average league attendance and the highest attendance at a single game. The fifth, the number of teams per million inhabitants, shows how widespread university hockey is in a country and carries 10 %. Each criterion is scored from 0 to 100 against the best competition in the ranking. Attendance uses a logarithmic scale, so one record crowd does not push every other league towards zero. Europe and World are scored separately.

Missing data

Three competitions, BUIHA, JP-UNIV and Korea-Univ, do not publish attendance figures, so they score zero for those criteria. Several other values are estimates from public sources and are marked on the ranking page. ECAA invites leagues to publish their attendance and to send corrections, so that the next edition rests on complete official data.

A new European record

The 2025/2026 ranking closes just as a new season sets a new benchmark. On 8 October 2026, 10,000 spectators filled Mustang Aréna in Ostrava for the ULLH derby between VŠB – Technical University of Ostrava and the University of Ostrava, a new European record for university hockey. The game belongs to the 2026/2027 season and will count towards next year's ranking. The world record remains 104,173, set at Michigan Stadium in 2010.

Next edition

The College Hockey Ranking is published once a year after the season ends. The 2026/2027 edition will follow the end of the coming season.
