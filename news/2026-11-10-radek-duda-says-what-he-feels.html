Radek Duda: a player who says what he feels

In Czech hockey, few have the guts to speak as openly as he does.

ECAA · 10 November 2026

Hockey has a language of its own: "we take it one game at a time", "credit to the other team", "we just have to work harder". Safe answers, said a thousand times. Radek Duda never spoke that language.

The former forward has always said what he feels, about the game, about players and about the people who run the sport, even when it was not the popular thing to say. In Czech hockey that takes real guts, and hardly anyone else has them.

A career on the big stage

Born in 1979, Duda was drafted by the Calgary Flames in 1998 and played junior hockey in Canada with the Regina Pats and the Lethbridge Hurricanes in the WHL. Back home he played for HC Sparta Praha, HC Plzeň, HC Slavia Praha and HC Energie Karlovy Vary, and abroad for Ak Bars Kazan, the Malmö Redhawks, Ilves, SCL Tigers Langnau, EHC Basel, EHC Freiburg and HK Dukla Trenčín. More than two decades of professional hockey in six countries.

Why it matters

Sport needs people who are not afraid to speak. Honest opinions start debates, and debates move the game forward. They also make fans care. That is true in the Extraliga and it is just as true in university hockey, where players and clubs are still finding their voice.

You do not have to agree with everything Radek Duda says. But you always know where he stands. ECAA says: respect.
