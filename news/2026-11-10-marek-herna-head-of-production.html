Marek Herna appointed Head of Production

He will lead game-day entertainment for university hockey in Europe.

ECAA · 10 November 2026

The European College Athletics Association (ECAA) has appointed Marek Herna as Head of Production. In the new role he is responsible for entertainment at university hockey games across Europe.

From world championships to university rinks

Herna works at Go4Gold Czech Republic, a Prague-based fan experience agency. Go4Gold designs and runs the show around the game: arena entertainment, audiovisual content for big screens and on-site production. Its first project was video content for the 2015 IIHF Ice Hockey World Championship in the Czech Republic.

Since 2019, together with its partner Livebros, the agency has produced the game experience at IIHF World Championships in the Czech Republic, Slovakia and Latvia. At the 2023 FIBA Basketball World Cup it delivered the fan experience in five arenas in the Philippines, Indonesia and Japan: 92 games in front of more than 700,000 spectators. Herna brings this experience from top international events to university hockey.

Two goals

Herna's task is to deliver the best possible experience to fans at every game, from the moment they arrive at the rink to the final buzzer. At the same time, he will help clubs turn what happens in the arena into income, so that game-day activities support the clubs' budgets.

Why now

University hockey in Europe is drawing bigger crowds than ever. On 8 October 2026, 10,000 spectators filled Mustang Aréna in Ostrava for a ULLH derby, a new European record for university hockey, and the ULLH averaged 607 spectators per game in the 2025/2026 season. With more fans coming to the rink, the show around the game matters more for both the audience and the clubs.
