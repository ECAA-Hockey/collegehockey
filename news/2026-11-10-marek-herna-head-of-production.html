Marek Herna appointed ULLH Head of Production

He will lead game-day entertainment in European university hockey.

ECAA · 10 November 2026

University hockey in Europe has its crowds. Now it gets the show to match. The Czech university hockey league ULLH (Univerzitní liga ledního hokeje) has appointed Marek Herna as its Head of Production, responsible for entertainment in European university hockey.

His brief is simple to say and hard to do: give every fan the best night out they can have at a hockey game, and help clubs turn that night into revenue. Few people are better prepared for it. Herna has spent more than ten years where marketing, sports brands and live events meet, from a global consumer goods company to world championships, and he has been part of university hockey since the day he helped found a club.

Built a university club from scratch

In 2015 Herna co-founded ČVUT Engineers Prague, the university hockey club of the Czech Technical University. He built the club's brand, its marketing and PR and, above all, its fan community. He then joined the ULLH as marketing manager and later moved to fan experience and production, where he produces the league's biggest games.

Six years at Unilever

Before sport became his full-time job, Herna spent six years at Unilever, one of the world's largest consumer goods companies, in marketing, trade marketing and brand management. He holds a bachelor's degree in National Economics and a master's degree in Management from the Prague University of Economics and Business (VŠE).

From the Extraliga to world championships

Since 2023 Herna has been a project manager at Go4Gold Czech Republic, the Prague fan experience agency that builds the show around some of the biggest tournaments in world sport. Go4Gold's first project was video content for the 2015 IIHF World Championship in the Czech Republic, and it has produced the game experience at IIHF World Championships ever since.

His own list of projects reads like a who's who of Czech and international sport:

  • Hockey: fan experience and arena entertainment for HC Sparta Praha and HC Energie Karlovy Vary, the rebranding of Bílí Tygři Liberec (new visual identity, content strategy and content), and game production for the Czech national team.
  • IIHF World Championships: arena content management at the World Championships in the Czech Republic and in Switzerland, and content production at the IIHF Women's World Championship.
  • Basketball: arena entertainment and content at the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2023, where Go4Gold ran the fan experience for 92 games in five arenas across the Philippines, Indonesia and Japan, in front of more than 700,000 spectators and a record opening-night crowd of 38,155. Also FIBA Women's EuroBasket and the FIBA U19 Women's Basketball World Cup, with a LED court and digital content on the floor.
  • Football and campaigns: the LFA Media Day 2026 for the Czech professional football league, the creative concept and script for the LFA Fantasy campaign, TV spots for Fortuna, and a campaign film for Czech women's ice hockey that takes on stereotypes about women in the sport.

That is the level of production Herna now brings to university rinks.

What changes at the rink

Herna is in charge of everything a fan sees and hears beyond the game itself: how the night starts, what happens on the big screen and in the stands, and how teams, sponsors and the crowd come together. The goal is that a university game feels like an event worth coming back for, not just a match.

Game night as a business

The second half of the job is just as important. Herna will help clubs monetise what happens in the arena, so that a full house and a good show also support the clubs' budgets. For student-run clubs that is often the difference between surviving and growing.

Why now

The timing is no accident. On 8 October 2026, 10,000 spectators filled Mustang Aréna in Ostrava for a ULLH derby, a new European record for university hockey. A year earlier, 9,319 fans came to the Battle of Prague. The ULLH averaged 607 spectators per game in the 2025/2026 season, leads the ECAA College Hockey Ranking in Europe and ranks second in the world behind NCAA Division I. This season the league has added a second division with four new teams and moved its broadcasts to Oneplay.

More fans, more teams and more cameras mean higher expectations. With Herna, ULLH wants to meet them, and to set the standard for game-day entertainment in university hockey across Europe.
