Marek Herna appointed ULLH Head of Production

He will lead game-day entertainment in European university hockey.

ECAA · 10 November 2026

University hockey in Europe has its crowds. Now it gets the show to match. The Czech university hockey league ULLH (Univerzitní liga ledního hokeje) has appointed Marek Herna as its Head of Production, responsible for entertainment in European university hockey.

His brief is simple to say and hard to do: give every fan the best night out they can have at a hockey game, and help clubs turn that night into revenue.

A pro from the world's biggest arenas

Herna comes from Go4Gold Czech Republic, the Prague fan experience agency that builds the show around some of the biggest tournaments in world sport. Go4Gold creates arena entertainment, audiovisual content for big screens and on-site production, and has worked in hockey from the very start: its first project was video content for the 2015 IIHF Ice Hockey World Championship in the Czech Republic.

Since 2019, together with its partner Livebros, the agency has produced the game experience at IIHF World Championships in the Czech Republic, Slovakia and Latvia. In 2023 it beat large American and Australian competitors to deliver the fan experience at the FIBA Basketball World Cup: 92 games in five arenas across the Philippines, Indonesia and Japan, more than 700,000 spectators, and a record opening-night crowd of 38,155 at the Philippine Arena.

That is the level of production Herna now brings to university rinks.

What changes at the rink

Herna is in charge of everything a fan sees and hears beyond the game itself: how the night starts, what happens on the big screen and in the stands, and how teams, sponsors and the crowd come together. The goal is that a university game feels like an event worth coming back for, not just a match.

Game night as a business

The second half of the job is just as important. Herna will help clubs monetise what happens in the arena, so that a full house and a good show also support the clubs' budgets. For student-run clubs that is often the difference between surviving and growing.

Why now

The timing is no accident. On 8 October 2026, 10,000 spectators filled Mustang Aréna in Ostrava for a ULLH derby, a new European record for university hockey. A year earlier, 9,319 fans came to the Battle of Prague. The ULLH averaged 607 spectators per game in the 2025/2026 season, leads the ECAA College Hockey Ranking in Europe and ranks second in the world behind NCAA Division I. This season the league has added a second division with four new teams and moved its broadcasts to Oneplay.

More fans, more teams and more cameras mean higher expectations. With Herna, ULLH wants to meet them, and to set the standard for game-day entertainment in university hockey across Europe.
